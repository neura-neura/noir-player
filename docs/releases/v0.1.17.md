Noir Player 0.1.17 adds on-screen feedback for playback controls and remembers your playback speed between sessions.

## Playback controls

- Left and Right Arrow seek backward or forward by 5 seconds. A centered overlay shows the destination time, including when a seek reaches the beginning or end of the video.
- Added speed controls to the playback bar. Press D to increase speed by 0.1, S to decrease it by 0.1, or R to return to normal speed. The selected speed is restored after restarting the app.
- Press C to show or hide subtitles, M to mute or unmute, and Up or Down Arrow to adjust volume.
- Press Ctrl+- or Ctrl++ to decrease or increase subtitle font size.
- Speed, volume, mute, subtitle visibility, and subtitle size changes display centered feedback.
- The pointer hides after 2.2 seconds of inactivity over the player and reappears when moved.

## Fixes

- Playback shortcuts leave text entry and modified arrow shortcuts alone.
- Subtitle timing uses the current media position after seeking.
- Delayed volume events no longer replace feedback for a more recent action.

## Windows download

Use the x64 setup executable for the standard installation, or the MSI package for an MSI-based installation. This release also includes signed updater artifacts for in-app updates.
